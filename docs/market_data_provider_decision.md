# Market data provider decision

Public documentation checked on **2026-11-20 UTC**. This date applies to the source links below. **Verified** means stated in a named public source. **Conditional** means there is a plausible fit subject to the actual entitlement, terms, or data checks. **Unresolved** means the public material did not establish the claim. The assessment used documentation and pricing pages, with no market-data API calls, downloads, or account changes.

## Research requirements

The empirical dataset needs daily SPY, QQQ, and IWM OHLCV for observed US exchange sessions from 2010-01-01 through 2025-12-31, plus at least 63 earlier completed observations wherever scoring is expected to start at the first eligible 2010 session. The engine uses completed closes for signal history and whole-share sizing, then models fills at the next eligible observed open and marks positions at raw closes. Its five-interval and 63-interval signals count observations. A missing session can change both execution timing and the signal schedule. The data contract checks order, duplicates, and OHLCV validity and accepts gaps and a provider-declared price basis. Execution, planning, backtest, and chronology require `RAW`. ## Timestamp and price provenance

- **Massive** identifies the start of an aggregate window with Unix milliseconds in `t` and documents Eastern Time bucketing. Convert to the exchange-local session date and verify the eligible-trade rules for daily open/close, especially extended hours and early closes. Corporate-action dates are event fields. Declaration date is useful but has no announcement time or historic revision chain. The current flat-file timetable targets about 11:00 ET the next day, so it cannot substantiate availability before the next regular opening. Check the separate REST publication basis for that assumption. [Custom bars](https://massive.com/docs/rest/stocks/aggregates/custom-bars) · [Extended hours](https://massive.com/knowledge-base/article/market-data-outside-of-normal-hours) · [Flat-file timing](https://massive.com/knowledge-base/article/what-time-are-massives-flat-files-ready-each-day)
- **Alpha Vantage** supplies daily date keys and separate action events. Confirm exchange timezone, actual daily availability, pay dates, and revisions. **Sharadar SFP** calls `date` the trade date and publishes an update schedule. ACTIONS has a dated event record. Neither the date nor the update schedule proves the first appearance of a particular row. **Yahoo** uses the exchange-local date in the current adapter, taking it from the returned index. Action values joined to those rows do not establish payment dates or original publication time. **Stooq** timing semantics remain unresolved. [Alpha Vantage API](https://www.alphavantage.co/documentation/) · [Sharadar Fund Prices](https://sharadar.com/docs/funds) · [Sharadar ACTIONS](https://sharadar.com/docs/actions) · [Yahoo adapter](../src/trading_research/yahoo_history.py)
- Record retrieval UTC, licensed source and endpoint, sanitized request parameters, adjustment evidence, rule/schema version, coverage and quality counts, and payload hashes. A retrieval timestamp records when today's vintage was captured. An effective date records when an action applies. Both can differ from when a strategy could have known the information. Corrections can revise historical rows, and a later split can restate the adjusted history. No reviewed daily product established complete historical vintages with first-distribution and revision timestamps for this study. [Existing manifest](../src/trading_research/yahoo_history.py) · [Massive adjustment FAQ](https://massive.com/knowledge-base/article/is-massives-stock-data-adjusted-for-splits-or-dividends)

## Decision and next step

**Primary candidate, conditional — Massive Stocks Advanced.** It has the strongest documented combination of direct unadjusted ETF bars, action dates and ratios, and sufficient plan depth. Verify permission for persistent private snapshots and the intended benchmark/report before any subscription or acquisition. **Backup candidate, conditional — Alpha Vantage Premium.** It has documented raw daily bars at a lower advertised entry price. Qualify it only after confirming all three symbols' coverage, dividend pay dates and share basis, opening-price convention, and storage/publication rights. Sharadar SFP's reconstructed raw open, inaccessible Stooq specifications, and Yahoo's adjusted basis make those weaker fits for the current execution contract.

The existing OHLCV record can stay focused on prices and volume. A real empirical implementation needs three additions.

1. **Separate corporate actions and accounting.** Apply effective split ratios to held shares and reconcile pending quantities before trading on the new share basis. Define fractional shares and cash-in-lieu. Record cash distributions separately with entitlement, ex-date, pay date, and amount on the correct share basis. Track earned receivables in equity and credit spendable cash on payment under an explicit policy. Reconcile special distributions and avoid double counting by valuing positions at raw marks. The current ledger changes shares and cash through fills only. [Portfolio ledger](../src/trading_research/portfolio.py)
2. **Separate signal and execution price conventions.** Adjust lookback comparisons for splits effective through the decision date so a mechanical share-basis change cannot create a signal. Specify whether indicators use price returns or total returns. Keep as-traded opens and closes for sizing, fills, and valuation. Split-adjusted OHLC normalizes share units. Dividend-adjusted series or an adjusted close incorporate distribution assumptions and cannot supply as-traded opens.
3. **Validate and preserve licensed snapshots.** Check every expected exchange session, inclusive endpoints, timezone, early closes, missing/extra rows, and split/dividend reconciliations before accepting the source. Missing expected sessions require investigation because replay advances to the next supplied opening. Place entitled payloads and caches in a private, Git-ignored location on D: and apply the actual retention agreement. Keep public repository fixtures synthetic.

The next implementation step is a **licensed data qualification**. Obtain written answers on the exact endpoint, all three ETFs plus warm-up coverage, regular-session OHLC, action timing, storage duration, and publication allowances. Once entitled and authorized, qualify a small private snapshot against a known action and an exchange calendar before designing the adapter or running empirical comparisons.
