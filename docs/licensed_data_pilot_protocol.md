# Licensed Massive SPY data pilot protocol

## Readiness and scope

**Acquisition is blocked pending licensing evidence.** This protocol qualifies SPY daily market data for **2024-01-01 through 2024-12-31, inclusive**, once a capture is authorized. It is an integration pilot. Its outputs are data acceptance decisions and reproducibility records. Public terms and documentation were reviewed on 2026-11-22. Live response compatibility and economic correctness remain unverified.

The [offline adapter](../src/trading_research/massive_history.py) parses one saved daily response and converts individual supported split records. The [preflight audit](../src/trading_research/data_audit.py) checks supplied rows, dates, actions and source hashes. The repository has [synthetic adapter tests](../tests/test_massive_history.py) and [audit tests](../tests/test_data_audit.py). The [backtest](../src/trading_research/backtest.py) accepts supplied `corporate_actions`, but the [chronological evaluator](../src/trading_research/chronology.py) currently takes SPY bars and no action input. The [snapshot preflight](spy_snapshot_preflight.md) handles reviewed ordinary USD dividend records. Action-aware chronological evaluation remains a separate integration gap. The [provider decision](market_data_provider_decision.md) records the broader source comparison.

## Licensing gate

Keep the checklist open while any material permission is unresolved. Record the applicable order, plan, source agreements, term version, written answers, and who confirmed them. Apply the source-specific terms where they control. **Documented** describes the published rule. **Conditional** requires account, plan or source evidence. **Unresolved** requires an answer before acquisition. [Massive Market Data Terms §§1–5, 8–9](https://massive.com/legal/market-data-terms-of-service) · [Massive Individuals Terms](https://massive.com/legal/individuals-terms-of-service)

| Permission or restriction | Evidence required before acquisition |
| --- | --- |
| Automated API access | **Conditional.** Confirm that authorized REST automation, the three specified endpoints, SPY's 2024 history, and the applicable rate limits are covered by the intended account and source agreements. [REST Quickstart](https://massive.com/docs/rest) |
| Personal research and nonprofessional eligibility | **Conditional.** Confirm the intended use is personal and nonbusiness and document eligibility under each applicable source's nonprofessional rules. These are separate conditions under Market Data Terms §§1–2. |
| Local snapshot storage | **Conditional.** Confirm that the entitlement permits a private copy on D: and covers the source payloads, normalized bars, and working copies needed for this protocol. |
| Retention after entitlement ends | **Documented restriction.** §8 generally requires stopping use and deleting affected copies promptly and within 30 days. Downgrades can remove older history from entitlement. Stricter source rules may apply. Inactive backup and legal retention exceptions have specific limits. Obtain written terms for any longer usable archive needed by the project, or record an acceptable licensed retention and purge plan before acquiring data. |
| Benchmark analysis | **Unresolved.** §5(d) requires separate written licensing for specified benchmark uses. Obtain written scope for the intended private strategy-versus-buy-and-hold comparison and any later publication, including applicable third-party approval where required. |
| Publication of derived statistics | **Unresolved.** §5(c) permits private use of qualifying analyses that contain no licensed information and cannot reconstruct or substitute for it. Obtain written permission for the specific statistics and release channel. |
| Publication of research reports | **Unresolved.** Confirm which tables, figures and prose may appear in a portfolio report, including whether a public repository or website is permitted. |
| Redistribution of raw and normalized data | **Documented restriction.** Treat normalized rows as licensed information and keep both forms private. Any later release needs express rights for the material and audience. |
| Storage of derived corporate-action records | **Unresolved.** Confirm whether normalized split ratios, dividend dates and amounts, and reconciliation files may be stored, for how long, and what must be purged. Treat row-level records as licensed until scope is confirmed. |

## Pilot inputs and reproducibility

- **Prices.** Capture the original Massive Stocks response for `/v2/aggs/ticker/SPY/range/1/day/2024-01-01/2024-12-31` with explicit `adjusted=false`, ascending order and a recorded limit sufficient for the window. Verify entitlement for the exact dates. Reserve the bars for data qualification. [Custom Bars](https://massive.com/docs/rest/stocks/aggregates/custom-bars)
- **Actions.** Capture all successful results for SPY from `/stocks/v1/splits` by execution date and `/stocks/v1/dividends` by ex-date within 2024. Keep payment dates as reported even when they cross year-end. Preserve every action page and pagination outcome, including a successful empty split result if applicable. [Splits](https://massive.com/docs/rest/stocks/corporate-actions/splits) · [Dividends](https://massive.com/docs/rest/stocks/corporate-actions/dividends)
- **Session evidence.** Build a dated, ordered NYSE Arca session list for the full interval from authoritative evidence, including holidays, short sessions and any exceptional closure notices. SPY is listed on NYSE Arca. Include the July 3, November 29 and December 24 early closes as sessions with their actual closing hours. Retain the source and version of the calendar. [State Street SPY listing](https://www.ssga.com/us/en/intermediary/etfs/state-street-spdr-sp-500-etf-trust-spy) · [NYSE Group calendar](https://ir.theice.com/press/news-details/2022/NYSE-Group-Announces-2023-2024-and-2025-Holiday-and-Early-Closings-Calendar/default.aspx)

Use an access-controlled, Git-ignored `data/` location on D: if allowed by the applicable terms. Assign a snapshot ID and log the instrument, sanitized request URL and parameters, status and request IDs, response flags and counts, UTC retrieval time, relative file paths, and SHA-256 of each original response body before parsing. Record the adapter Git commit, Python and time-zone data versions, calendar evidence, reconciliation sources, and each finding's disposition. Keep credentials out of the capture record. Keep each permitted revision as a distinct snapshot. A source-byte hash identifies a particular capture. Its retrieval time identifies when that vintage was obtained. Apply the retention terms to snapshots, normalized records, reconciliation files and caches.

## Live-response validation and acceptance

1. **Check source bytes and response shape.** Recompute each manifest digest and reject any mismatch. Pass the parsed prices to `parse_daily_aggregates` with its actual saved credential-free `request_url`, `expected_symbol="SPY"`, `adjustment=PriceAdjustment.RAW`, and recorded UTC `retrieved_at`. Require `status="OK"`, matching ticker and Boolean `adjusted=false`, a request ID, valid counts, and rows carrying `t/o/h/l/c/v`. The current adapter rejects an empty, delayed, paginated or malformed bar response. Resolve any unexpected envelope with Massive before accepting it. Preserve the request and the provider-reported RAW label in provenance. [Custom Bars response](https://massive.com/docs/rest/stocks/aggregates/custom-bars)
2. **Check session and OHLCV integrity.** Confirm that each integer millisecond `t` resolves to local midnight in `America/New_York`. Check boundaries on both sides of the DST changes and compare the complete date set with the independent calendar. Add the price `source_path`, `source_sha256` and `snapshot_id` to `capture.dataset` using `dataclasses.replace`. Call `audit_etf_data` with the dataset, `expected_sessions`, inclusive `calendar_range`, and reconciled `corporate_actions`. Verify action and calendar file hashes separately. Require every expected session exactly once and finite, valid OHLCV. Quarantine datasets with missing, extra, unordered or duplicate observations and preserve the original rows. [Massive timestamp guide](https://massive.com/knowledge-base/article/how-should-i-go-about-reporting-a-possible-massive-data-discrepancy)
3. **Check economic meaning independently.** Obtain the feed and eligible-trade definition for each price field and volume. Confirm that the reported opens and closes fit the simulator's intended regular-session execution and marking convention. Compare the first and last sessions, early closes, sessions around each action, and every discrepancy with an authorized independent reference on the same price and volume basis. Record source, field, session, published precision and any difference. Require differences to be reconciled from documented definitions or corrections. Confirm historical delivery and correction policies separately before using a bar to justify next-session execution. The midnight label provides the session date. Historical availability requires its own evidence. [Custom Bars trade eligibility](https://massive.com/docs/rest/stocks/aggregates/custom-bars) · [Massive extended hours](https://massive.com/knowledge-base/article/market-data-outside-of-normal-hours)
4. **Decide from the evidence.** Record adapter compatibility, integrity, calendar completeness, economic price basis and action completeness separately. Accept the snapshot as pilot-qualified only when there are no blocking findings, all warnings are reviewed, and independent evidence resolves every qualification-critical question. Keep the original finding severities. In particular, `raw_provenance_unverified` remains in the audit output and any independent resolution belongs in the separate evidence record. A reviewed payment-after-coverage warning can coexist with acceptance if the payment date and amount are independently confirmed. Leave a missing document or evidence gap unresolved and withhold acceptance.

## Corporate-action reconciliation and dividend blocker

Match the entire returned action set to issuer notices, filings or exchange records. An empty split result is acceptable only with evidence of a successful, complete date-filtered response and independent confirmation that SPY had no applicable splits in the period. For each split, reconcile ticker, effective session, action type and the exact new-to-old share ratio independently before calling `parse_split` on the record. Check for the matching effective-session bar and retain unsupported events as open findings. Avoid treating a price discontinuity as an event.

For each distribution, verify the stable ID, USD denomination, ordinary cash classification, ex-date, payment date and exact per-share amount. Reconcile omissions and revisions with issuer records. Include payments due after the window for ex-dates within it. Verify the action has an observed ex-session and the preceding eligible close. If an action falls on the first bar, obtain separately authorized prior-session context and qualify it with its own request and hash. Check actions against an audit view that includes the preceding close, with matching expanded calendar and request bounds. Preserve the separate 2024 integrity report. [State Street fund and distribution documents](https://www.ssga.com/us/en/institutional/etfs/state-street-spdr-500-etf-trust-spy)

The [offline preflight](spy_snapshot_preflight.md) implements a narrow mapping for reviewed ordinary USD events with a confirmed prior-close share basis. Automatic conversion of unreconciled live responses remains blocked. Massive's `cash_amount` is the as-paid amount per share. `split_adjusted_cash_amount` uses a restated share basis. [`CashDividend`](../src/trading_research/corporate_actions.py) expects a positive ordinary USD amount on the share basis of the preceding observed close because entitlement uses the holdings captured at that close. If a split shares the ex-date or affects the units used in payment, applying the as-paid amount directly to those holdings can overstate or understate the receivable. Confirm the unit convention and event classification with Massive and independently reconcile it before constructing the accounting input. [Massive as-paid amounts](https://massive.com/knowledge-base/article/does-massive-adjust-historic-dividends-for-splits) · [Massive dividend fields](https://massive.com/docs/rest/stocks/corporate-actions/dividends)

A later automatic mapper requires three acceptance cases.

1. An ordinary USD record with no share-basis change matches the issuer's exact ex-date, pay date and per-share amount and produces the expected entitlement for known preceding-close shares.
2. A documented overlapping split and distribution case yields the independently reconciled prior-close amount and cash entitlement for known holdings. If no such case appears in the pilot, obtain an independently verified example before enabling general conversion.
3. Missing or conflicting dates, an unsupported distribution type or currency, a duplicate action, or an unresolved share basis fails visibly. The mapper preserves the source record and supplies no accounting event for that case.
