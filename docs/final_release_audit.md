## Adversarial and numerical checks

- **Order lifecycle.** Reaffirming a target during a gap preserved the original accepted order. A smaller target cancelled the ten-share order during the gap and sized a five-share replacement at the later $80 close. It filled at the following $90 open. The $1 fee left $549 cash. A same-day flat signal preserved that morning's committed fill and left a separate accepted exit. An invalid cancellation batch rolled back its earlier staged changes. A rejected mean-reversion exit raised `MeanReversionExitRejected`, while an exit awaiting a later open remained accepted and visible. [Planner](../src/trading_research/planning.py#L51) · [Opening and closing sequence](../src/trading_research/backtest.py#L204) · [Exit handling](../src/trading_research/mean_reversion.py#L54)
- **Benchmark boundaries.** A warm-up decline triggered standalone mean reversion. The common-window run suppressed that earlier decision, began all portfolios at $1,000 on the same anchor, and booked fills strictly afterward. A valid 11-share buy-and-hold entry at $89 with a $1.25 fee left $19.75 cash and $1,064.75 at the final $95 close. Its history contained one fill, with shares still held at the end. [Shared window](../src/trading_research/benchmark.py#L166)
- **Performance arithmetic.** For $1,000 → $1,100 → $990, the independent calculation gives +10% and −10%, sample variance `0.02`, per-interval deviation `sqrt(0.02)`, annualized volatility `sqrt(5.04) ≈ 224.4994%`, and zero Sharpe. The evaluator matched. Total return was −1%, drawdown 10%, gross committed notional $1,000, turnover `100/103`, and mean closing exposure `2/3`. Equal nonzero returns gave zero volatility and undefined Sharpe. Returns of −10% and −20% gave negative Sharpe, approximately −33.675. Two dated marks from $1,000 to $1,210 over 730 days gave 10% CAGR and undefined volatility. [Sampling and metrics](../src/trading_research/performance.py#L80)
- **Corporate actions.** A 2-for-1 split with a same-day $2 dividend per old share turned ten shares into twenty at a raw $49 close. The $20 receivable left equity at $1,000 and available cash at $0. The Saturday payment moved $20 into cash in the next observed portfolio snapshot with equity unchanged. Truncating on Friday retained the receivable in Friday equity, with the Saturday date appearing only in the payment audit. The ledger rejected spending Saturday's receipt in a Friday fill. A pending order crossing a split raised `PendingOrderSplitError`. Cancelling it beforehand allowed the action and produced no fill. A later split did not change an earlier momentum signal or fill. The analytical close of an earlier raw $100 bar became $50 only at the effective split. [Runner](../src/trading_research/backtest.py#L204) · [Action staging](../src/trading_research/coordinator.py#L80) · [Analytical close](../src/trading_research/signals.py#L49)
- **Provider evidence.** The Massive adapter rejected a missing request adjustment and a request/response flag mismatch. Its declared RAW result still produced `raw_provenance_unverified`. A properly declared split-adjusted response generated a blocking audit finding and the backtest refused it. [Offline adapter](../src/trading_research/massive_history.py#L100) · [Preflight](../src/trading_research/data_audit.py#L373)
