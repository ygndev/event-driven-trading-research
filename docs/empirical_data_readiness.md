# Empirical market data readiness

## Decision

**BLOCKED for empirical SPY backtesting.** The v0.1.1 engine can simulate on supplied RAW bars and supported corporate actions. The project still needs a license for the intended comparison and retention, a reconciled SPY snapshot with independently verified session and price conventions, and action-aware chronological comparisons. The audit used repository code, synthetic tests, and public documentation. It made no market-data requests or empirical runs.

**Massive is the first provider to qualify** because its daily RAW endpoint and separate actions fit the existing offline adapter. The lowest published individual subscription with sufficient bar history is Stocks Advanced at **US$199/month**. Ask about a lower-cost licensed snapshot before subscribing. Alpha Vantage Premium at **US$49.99/month** is the price comparator and backup if it can establish equivalent SPY coverage, action detail, and rights. Yahoo is unsuitable for the current RAW execution contract. These prices are access prices, with no confirmed all-in price for a publishable, reproducible pilot. [Massive Stocks pricing](https://massive.com/pricing?product=stocks) · [Alpha Vantage Premium](https://www.alphavantage.co/premium/)

### Rights and provider questions

- **Massive.** Individual terms cover private noncommercial research and allow entitled local storage subject to source rules. Business or organizational use requires the appropriate agreement. Market Data Terms §5 restricts sharing Information and permits retaining non-reconstructible personal analyses for personal use. §5(d) requires a separate written license for index or benchmark use. Obtain a written determination covering the specific private strategy-vs-buy-and-hold comparison, as well as any public tables or charts and required notices or attribution. Under §8 the paid license continues through the paid period. At downgrade, access contracts to qualifying lower-tier information. When rights end, stop use and delete affected payloads, transformed substitutes and caches promptly and within 30 days, subject to stricter source terms. Inactive backups have separate conditions and a 90-day ceiling. Per-request purchases have separate, nonperpetual retention terms. An ordinary month does not support ongoing use of the full 2009–2025 capture after downgrading to Basic. Preserve required notices. [Market Data Terms §§1, 4–9](https://massive.com/legal/market-data-terms-of-service) · [Individual terms](https://massive.com/legal/individuals-terms-of-service)
- **Alpha Vantage.** The public EULA describes private individual use and defines commercial use more broadly, including specified affiliations. Ask for the applicable data license for the intended user and project. The published terms do not establish private retention of licensed captures during or after cancellation, raw redistribution, publication of aggregate performance, or output-specific attribution. The user-content ownership clause does not settle rights in the source or derived outputs. [Terms §§2–7](https://www.alphavantage.co/terms_of_service/) · [Official support](https://www.alphavantage.co/support/api-key/)
- **Yahoo.** US terms require express prior permission for automated collection even for personal use. Manual offline CSV access is a product feature. Written entitlement is still needed for the planned automated route, long-term storage, derived publication and any applicable upstream terms or notices. No permission for a public repository release of data or statistics was established. [Yahoo US Terms](https://legal.yahoo.com/us/en/yahoo/terms/otos/index.html)
- **Ask before spending.** Can the named plan or one-off license deliver SPY as-traded EOD OHLCV for all required sessions (including at least 63 pre-2010 sessions) plus every split and ordinary or other distribution through the end of 2025? Confirm session and auction semantics, event types, pay dates, correction history and release times, completeness by endpoint, exact price and assent requirements. Separately ask who may access the copies, which response bodies, transformed records and backups may remain after cancellation and for how long, whether private simulated SPY-vs-buy-and-hold comparisons are covered, and which public non-reconstructible metrics or charts are allowed under the provider and source agreements. Ask for any required attribution, disclaimers, written approvals, deletion and display terms. Provider data, responses and reconstructible series stay private under the agreed rights.

## Canonical input and provenance

Keep the existing engine contracts. Bind them to an immutable private snapshot manifest and an independently sourced calendar before normalization feeds the engine.

| Input | Minimum evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Daily bars | Stable instrument and provider symbol, exchange session date in America/New_York, USD and venue/coverage convention, complete OHLCV, declared basis and separately reconciled RAW status. Preserve provider request and parameters, UTC acquisition time, original body SHA-256, and dataset version or explicit unavailable status. Validate chronological uniqueness, matching ranges, finite/positive and internally consistent OHLC, and integral nonnegative volume. Investigate zero-volume rows and verify the volume convention. Keep normalized hashes separately. |
| Splits | Stable issuer mapping, effective exchange session, exact new/old ratio from original numerator and denominator, forward/reverse classification, raw share basis, provider ID when available, and provenance pointing to the response record. Keep announcement, observed availability and revision dates when known. Use event ratios rather than cumulative backward factors. |
| Cash dividends | Stable action ID, exchange ex-date, actual pay date, original Decimal cash amount per share, currency, classification, declaration/record dates when provided, and provider provenance and revisions. Require a confirmed ordinary USD event and verify the prior-close share basis. Keep missing values explicit and block adaptation when a required field is missing. |

The manifest should identify the snapshot, code commit, schema, Python and timezone versions, instrument map and intended dates, including warm-up. For each bars, split, dividend and reconciliation input, record the exact sanitized path and parameters, inclusive span, provider/plan and version, request IDs, response flags, pagination lineage and row/page counts, acquisition UTC timestamp, relative file reference and SHA-256 of original bytes. Add paired normalized hashes, the calendar identifier, source, version, date range and hash, and the numerical and action reconciliation records. Record the chosen vintage, links to earlier versions and change dispositions, validation results, unresolved warnings, final gate decision, license reference, permitted outputs, and retention/review dates. ### Price and action reconciliation

- Confirm the Massive request and returned `adjusted=false` and independently reconcile representative raw opens and closes, the first and last covered sessions, large moves, and around action dates against an authorized independent source. Establish which consolidated trades and auctions set the modeled open and close, how early closes and extended hours are treated, and when a clean bar is available before the next modeled open. An ET-midnight anchor labels a session. Late prints can revise bars. Freeze approved vintages and record any corrections with both acquisition and effective dates. If market-time availability or revision behavior could change the decision and cannot be established, block the point-in-time study. [Massive adjustments](https://massive.com/knowledge-base/article/is-massives-stock-data-adjusted-for-splits-or-dividends) · [Trade eligibility](https://massive.com/knowledge-base/article/how-does-massive-create-the-open-high-low-close-volume-aggregate-bars) · [Late aggregates](https://massive.com/blog/aggregate-bar-delays)
- Collect both complete action streams under the approved license and independently reconcile dates, amounts and types with issuer or exchange records over warm-up and scoring, including an explicit check of any empty result. Preserve declaration versus effective dates and provider revision IDs or their absence. Massive offers optional original cash amount, currency and pay date fields and recurrence classifications that include recurring, special, supplemental, irregular and unknown. Recurrence alone cannot substantiate ordinary treatment. Use the original as-paid amount on the verified pre-split share basis. Keep split-adjusted cash amounts as reconciliation evidence only. [Massive splits](https://massive.com/docs/rest/stocks/corporate-actions/splits) · [Massive dividends](https://massive.com/docs/rest/stocks/corporate-actions/dividends)
- Fail closed for unsupported stock distributions, unclassified or special distributions, non-USD amounts, missing pay dates, conflicting revisions or duplicate dates that the current contracts cannot represent, and fractional split outcomes or pending orders spanning a split. Never impute a pay date from ex-date or record date. Keep actual payment dates even on a closed day. Confirm every in-window ex-date has a prior observed close. When payment is after the period cutoff, keep the receivable in the scored NAV and prevent the later cash from funding an earlier fill. Use explicit distributions with RAW prices only. Keep future-effective splits out of prior signal history. [Action contracts](../src/trading_research/corporate_actions.py) · [Backtest action handling](../src/trading_research/backtest.py) · [Split-history tests](../tests/test_split_history.py)

### Calendar verification

Supply a versioned ordered list of actual equity sessions for the entire requested range, with explicit inclusive calendar bounds, early-close session times and historically applicable exceptions. Compile and reconcile it from issuer/listing-venue identity, NYSE Group/Arca historical equity calendars and exchange notices. A current holiday table, weekdays, or a calendar inferred from the provider's returned rows cannot establish historical coverage. Confirm scheduled holidays and weekends as closed, preserve shortened sessions as valid once verified, and cite ad hoc closures and full-session halts. The validator checks date differences. Investigate absent expected bars against exchange records, entitlement boundaries, provider status or outage evidence, and any symbol halt or no-eligible-trade explanation. A genuine unrepresentable trading interruption still blocks this protocol. Extra dates, partial calendar coverage and unresolved gaps fail the gate. Keep an evidence-backed session file passed into the existing audit and use the exact same set for each comparison. A new calendar library is unnecessary for this one-instrument pilot. [NYSE hours and calendars](https://www.nyse.com/trade/hours-calendars) · [Example historical calendar](https://www.nyse.com/publicdocs/ICE_NYSE_2025_Yearly_Trading_Calendar.pdf) · [Calendar audit](../src/trading_research/data_audit.py)

## Acceptance gates

| Gate | Evidence required for READY |
| --- | --- |
| Rights | Record written or otherwise explicit applicable terms for acquisition, the intended private comparison, storage and deletion, and the lawful reproduction period. Keep publication separately blocked until the exact permitted outputs and attribution are approved. |
| Provenance and RAW | Complete, credential-free manifest with all input hashes verified, stable vintage, reconciled as-traded bars and validated session and availability convention. |
| Coverage | Full independently validated session list, exact matching observations through warm-up and final test, valid OHLCV and no unexplained missing, extra or duplicate records. |
| Actions | Reconciled split/dividend coverage and classifications, effective and pay dates and original share basis. Validate required fields before conversion. Use the same verified actions and boundary policy for strategy and benchmark valuation. |
| Timing and research design | At least 63 earlier closes for the intended anchors, cutoffs on all inputs, independent equal initial capital and locked parameters/costs. Demonstrate that later prices, corrections, and action terms cannot affect earlier decisions and that any pending exits stay explicit. |
| Review | Zero unresolved critical findings. A required provenance or completeness check reported as unverified is blocking for this protocol. Keep a written disposition for other warnings. |

**READY** requires evidence for every gate for the specified snapshot and use. **BLOCKED** means any gate is missing, fails, or has unresolved critical evidence. A clean synthetic test suite or a passing format check cannot waive a failed gate.
